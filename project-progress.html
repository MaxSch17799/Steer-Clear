Project Progress

Our validated MVP and the path forward

The Current MVP

Our current MVP system has been fully validated against the requirements we set out at the start of the project. It has proven to be fast in response, reliable in operation, and modular for easy installation across different ground service vehicles. The system enables safer and more efficient docking, even in scenarios with a reduced crew.

The Current Ground Handling Procedure

In today’s operations, docking a ground vehicle close to an aircraft requires multiple crew members and relies heavily on manual communication and visual checks. This process is time-consuming and can be error-prone, especially in busy or low-visibility conditions.

How Steer-Clear Improves This

Our MVP shows how docking can be faster and safer by providing real-time awareness directly to the operator. This reduces dependency on multiple ground staff and makes it possible to perform docking operations with smaller crews or even a single operator.

With clear cues and automated monitoring, Steer-Clear helps prevent costly damage while also improving turnaround efficiency for operators.

Next Steps

The next stage for Steer-Clear is to conduct in-field testing with operational vehicles to further validate performance under real-world conditions. At the same time, the design of the next prototype is already underway, building on the learnings of this MVP to improve robustness, usability, and integration.
